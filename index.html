About me

I lead Design Technology as a business function, not a help desk.

Over twelve years I have moved from documenting towers in Revit to setting the national strategy that decides how an entire practice models, coordinates and delivers. Through to July 2026 I established and ran the Design Technology and BIM function across three studios at Bates Smart, supporting approximately 270 staff, functionally leading 14 BIM coordinators, specialists, technicians and reporting directly to Practice Directors on investment priorities, delivery risk and capability gaps.

The work I care about sits where strategy meets the model: governed standards people will actually follow, BIM gates that surface setup decisions before they become rework, a single source of truth instead of a shared drive, and automation that gives hours back to designers. I connect the full chain, from Forma and Rhino site design through Revit documentation into Autodesk Construction Cloud coordination and project delivery.

I advocate for the responsible adoption of AI, automation and data-driven workflows, assessed on whether they improve design quality, operational performance and the capability of the people doing the work.

Selected work

Design Technology in practice

Five systems built to hold up across studios, not just on one project.

01  ·  Knowledge

DT Insite Hub

Problem
Design Technology information was fragmented across multiple locations, so the answer depended on who you asked.
Control
A central SharePoint hub holding standards, procedures, approved tools, design guides and regulations, workflows, training and support behind one navigation.
Artefact
Curated libraries including a categorised typical drafting details set, each detail carrying drawing number, category, scale and revision.
Reach
A governed source of truth for approximately 270 staff, piloted in Brisbane before national rollout.

02  ·  Operating model

National BIM operating model

Problem
BIM support had been uneven across the Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane studios.
Control
Restructured the function into a coordinated national support model with clearer ownership, coordinated resourcing and cross-studio support.
Artefact
Consistent role expectations and a national resourcing model across three studios.
Reach
Approximately 270 staff supported; 14 BIM specialists functionally led.

03  ·  Governance

Phase 20 and Phase 30 BIM gates

Problem
BIM setup decisions were being made late, when they are most expensive to change.
Control
A mandatory BIM Discovery session at Phase 20, covering Revit setup, BIM resourcing, whether a BIM Execution Plan is required, survey information, project and true north alignment, interoperability and model submission requirements.
Decision
A mandatory BIM Brief session at Phase 30 design development, adding CDE setup, model division, LOD and information requirements, modelling and sheet strategy, responsibilities and BIM Execution Planning.
Artefact
A completed Project BIM Brief for each job, giving clarity on model strategy and split and better resourcing decisions. Both gates are embedded in the internal Project Smart and Audit Smart management system.

04  ·  Adoption

Technology adoption and governance

Problem
New tools arrive faster than a practice can absorb them, and ungoverned adoption leaves every studio on a different setup.
Control
An assessment and rollout path for each tool: Guardian for Revit for model health and standards enforcement, a practice Content Catalog for governed families, Nezo for embodied carbon calculation, and Earlymark to connect the master programme to the Revit model.
Artefact
An approved toolset with owners, rollout plans and demonstrations, rather than a list of licences.
Purpose
Put sustainability and programme data inside the model, where design decisions are actually made.

05  ·  Capability

Capability and training

Problem
Tools and standards only hold if people are brought with them.
Control
A curated in-house programme of 30 one-hour sessions spanning Revit, Forma, Rhino and SketchUp, covering new software, workflows, modelling technique and tested practice, run alongside regular Lunch and Learn demonstrations.
Artefact
In-house curriculum paired with a global e-training platform, so staff have both a guided path and self-directed depth.
Purpose
Lift baseline capability rather than concentrate it in a few specialists.
